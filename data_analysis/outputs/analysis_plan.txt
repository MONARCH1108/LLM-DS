ANALYSIS PLAN
=============

PLAN OBJECTIVE
--------------
- To analyze the distribution of the `Age` column for each unique combination of `Sex` and `Survived` status.

PLAN ASSUMPTIONS
----------------
- The columns `Age`, `Sex`, and `Survived` are the correct inputs for this analysis, as identified in the analysis understanding.
- The unique combinations of values in the `Sex` and `Survived` columns define the segments for analysis.
- An aggregation operation applied to the `Age` column within each segment is sufficient to represent its distribution for the purpose of this query.

PLAN STEPS
----------
Step 1:
- Description: Group the records by the unique combinations of values in the `Sex` and `Survived` columns. For each resulting group, apply an aggregation operation to the `Age` column.
- Input Columns: `Age`, `Sex`, `Survived`
- Operation Type: Grouping and Aggregation
- Expected Output: A materialized tabular dataset where each row corresponds to a unique combination of `Sex` and `Survived`. The dataset will include columns for `Sex`, `Survived`, and one or more new columns representing the summarized `Age` for that group.

PLAN CONSTRAINTS
----------------
- This plan describes only the structural operations; it does not specify the statistical measures (e.g., mean, median) to be used for the aggregation of `Age`.
- The analysis is descriptive and any observed relationships between `Age`, `Sex`, and `Survived` should not be interpreted as causal.
