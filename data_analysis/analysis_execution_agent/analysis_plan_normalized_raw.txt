OBJECTIVE
To produce an aggregated representation of track popularity for each unique combination of genre and country.

ASSUMPTIONS
ASSUMPTION_1
The columns genre, country, and popularity contain the necessary information to fulfill the analysis objective.
ASSUMPTION_2
Each record in the dataset represents a single track with a corresponding genre, country, and popularity value.
ASSUMPTION_3
The country column represents a meaningful geographical attribute associated with each track.

STEPS
STEP_1
DESCRIPTION: Group all records in the dataset based on their unique genre and country values.
OPERATION: GROUP
GROUP_BY_COLUMNS
- genre
- country

STEP_2
DESCRIPTION: For each group created in the previous step, apply an aggregation operation to the corresponding popularity values.
OPERATION: AGGREGATE
TARGET_COLUMN: popularity
AGGREGATION_FUNCTION: UNCERTAINTY(Not specified in the plan)
OUTPUT_COLUMN_NAME: aggregated_popularity

CONSTRAINTS
CONSTRAINT_1
The semantic meaning and scale of the popularity column are not defined in the provided inputs. The interpretation of the resulting aggregated values is therefore limited.
CONSTRAINT_2
The analysis does not consider any temporal information, such as release_date. The results represent an aggregation across the entire time span of the dataset.
